# AVEN Property — brand brief for agents and builders

Paste this into a system prompt, a design tool, or a briefing. Tokens and logos live beside it in `kit/`.

**What we are.** Boutique, residential-only property management for Bunbury and the South West, WA. Run by Cara Nash (licensee, 17 years in the local industry) and Nathan Katseli (business development, sales and marketing). Proactive, structured management with published service standards and written guarantees.

**The name.** AVEN — say *AY-ven*. From avenue (the road ahead, lined with structure), avenir (French: the future), haven (shelter), avens (a green wildflower). AVEN is the road ahead, and the calm at the end of it. Public brand: *AVEN Property*; descriptor *Property Management* beneath it on formal surfaces.

**Organising idea.** *Calm, by design.* Calm is the promise; structure is why it's true. Three proofs: published, guaranteed standards · always within reach (every enquiry answered within a business day) · the manager who stays (small book, no handovers).

**Lines — the hierarchy (round 3, open).** Category: *Structured property management*. Organising idea: open — candidates *Structured, not reactive* / *Managed to a standard* / *Property management, in writing* / *No surprises* / *Found early. Fixed early* / *Fewer properties, managed properly* / *Calm, by design*. Execution line (masthead): open — *Managing today. Building tomorrow* / *Property, with the future in mind* / *Where your property is going matters* / *Calm. Clear. Considered* / *Property management, in writing*. Proof line: open — *Published standards. Written guarantees* / *Answered. Inspected. Guaranteed*. Promise: *If we don't deliver, you don't pay* — service pages and proposals only, always with its reason. Compass: *Calm, by design* (the feeling the system produces). Taglines set straight, never italic, never an exclamation mark. The slash ( / ) pairs two halves, a space each side.

**Voice.** Two registers. *Calm* for anything owner-facing: plain English, specific over superlative, warm, unhurried. *Real-talk* for social and education content: Cara telling it straight — funny, candid, Reddit-literate. Both share the core (we, short sentences, Australian spelling, kind always) and the nevers (no hype words, no promised outcomes, no mocking tenants or owners, no competitor names, no ALL CAPS, no sarcasm in complaints). Full rules in `voice.json`.

**Colour.** Evening Pine: Ink #1C2B26 · Pine #2E4B3F · Rain #5B7E8C (the accent — the evening star in every logo) · Sage #ADBFAE (lines and grounds only) · Sage-text #6F8579 (captions, eyebrows) · Cloud #F3F5F1 (page) · Paper #FBFCFA (cards). Proportion makes it calm: mostly cloud and paper, pine for structure, one rain moment per surface. Amber #8A6D3B is internal-only (open decisions in working docs).

**Type.** Serif = EB Garamond (called 27/9; alias `AVEN Serif`) for the wordmark, taglines and display; sans = Nunito Sans (alias `AVEN Sans`) for body and UI. Sentence case; wide-tracked caps only for small labels. No bold serif headlines. Voice never leans on em dashes — commas and full stops do the work.

**Logos.** Cara's own A-frame logo (drawn in ChatGPT, `assets/cara/cara-a.jpg`) is the frontrunner while other concepts are explored; if it wins it is redrawn as vector in the family grammar. Behind it, the Avenue family — Cara's brief: trees either side, a house in the middle, a road running down to it. Fine-line geometric, single pine weight, one rain-blue evening star above the house in every logo. Fourteen drawings in `kit/marks/20-33`; three earlier logos in reserve (`01`, `02`, `12`). Small-format cut = The Lane (`32`, `icons/avenue-icon.svg`); app tile = `33`. Outlined SVGs; never re-type the wordmark. The folder keeps its historical name `kit/marks/`.

**Imagery.** Real photography or fine-line illustration only. Lead: documentary images of Cara at work across the service area. Support: single-weight line drawings in pine on cloud. Campaign register: calm interiors in evening light. No stock-looking stock; no generated images presented as real.

**The promise (customer-facing).** Six guarantees in writing — statement accuracy, vacancy, inspection, tenant placement, communication, leasing updates — plus published service standards and a no-exit-fee first three months. Fee amounts are never quoted in brand material; they live in the fee schedule.

**Don't.** Don't use "premium/passionate/exceptional/stress-free". Don't promise rent outcomes. Don't name competitors. Don't put the descriptor inside the logo. Don't italicise a tagline. Don't let amber reach a customer.
